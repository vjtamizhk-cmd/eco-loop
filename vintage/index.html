📖 CONTROLS & HOW TO PLAY

The Golden Rules of Period 5 Pen Fight

🎮 AIMING: Move your Mouse across the desk or use [A] / [D] or [◀ / ▶] Arrow Keys.

⚡ POWER FLICK: Press [SPACE] or [ENTER] when the oscillating needle reaches your desired power.

🌪️ SHOT TYPES:
• [Q] Spin Whip: Hits pen tail for high-speed helicopter spin sweeps.
• [W] Straight Drive: Hits pen center for straight linear push.

🎯 AUTO-LOCK: Press [L] to align directly with the target pen.

🎥 CAMERAS: Press [1] for Bench View, [2] for Top-Down, [3] for Pen Cam.

🏆 OBJECTIVE: Knock opponent pens off the wooden bench while keeping your own pen safely on the desk!
